About Me

I study how mental health services vary, who receives treatment, and how people fare over time. My research combines nationwide registry data and causal inference methods, with a particular focus on compulsory mental health care and neurodevelopmental disorders.

I work primarily in R, with an interest in data visualisation and research methods.
